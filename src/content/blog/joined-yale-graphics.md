I joined the Yale Graphics Group as a Postdoc Associate in June 2024. My research focuses on a virtual try-on system for jewelry using machine learning methods, under the guidance of [Prof. Holly Rushmeier](https://graphics.cs.yale.edu/people/holly-rushmeier).

The project is funded by TATA and Yale. Project page: [Jewelry Relighting](https://junzhoupro.github.io/jewelryrelighting/).
